## 1. Introduction
My favorite TV series is **From**, a sci-fi mystery horror series created by *John Griffin* and executive produced by the Russo brothers and Jeff Pinkner. The story is set in a nightmarish, enigmatic town in middle America that traps everyone who enters. 

> *"No matter where you're driving in the United States, once you see the fallen tree across the road, you can never leave."*

---

## 2. Key Genres & Themes
- **Mystery & Thriller**: Unraveling the nature of the town, the faraway trees, and the talismans.
- **Psychological Horror**: The mental toll of isolation and nocturnal monsters wearing human faces.
- **Survival Drama**: Community governance, rationing resources, and guarding against betrayal.

---

## 3. Main Characters
1. **Boyd Stevens** (*Harold Perrineau*) – The town sheriff and defacto leader trying to keep order and uncover a way out.
2. **Donna Raines** (*Elizabeth Saunders*) – The stern, compassionate leader of *Colony House*.
3. **Jim & Tabitha Matthews** (*Eion Bailey & Catalina Sandino Moreno*) – A family who crashed into town searching for truth.
4. **Victor** (*Scott McCord*) – The longest-surviving resident who has lived in the town since childhood.
5. **Jade Herrera** (*David Alpay*) – A software developer using analytical skills to decipher town symbols.

## 6. Series Lore & Trivia
To survive nighttime attacks, residents must hang a carved stone talisman inside an enclosed dwelling:
```text
[Talisman Rule]
Hang the talisman securely by the door or window.
Never open the door after sundown, no matter who is speaking outside.
```
